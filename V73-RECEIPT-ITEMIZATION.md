# Miles & Meals v73 — Receipt Itemization

Receipt OCR can surface likely line items. Travelers assign each detected item to one person or Everyone, then apply the itemized split.

The final card/converted amount remains the source of truth. Tax, service charge and other remaining amount is allocated proportionally across the item subtotals. Stored expense-item rows provide an auditable itemization while normal expense splits continue powering budgets and settlements.
