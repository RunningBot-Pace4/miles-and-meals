# Miles & Meals v71 — Invite & Onboarding

- Trip Owners can create expiring secure invitation links.
- Invite tokens are random and stored in Neon only as SHA-256 hashes.
- Share Sheet, copy link and QR are available; QR is generated locally in the browser and the invite token is not sent to a third-party QR service.
- Invite preview shows only trip name, destination, dates and inviter name before login.
- Login/register preserve the invite deep link and return the traveler to the join flow.
- Joining grants only that Trip/destination membership, activates the trip and routes to personal-budget onboarding when required.
