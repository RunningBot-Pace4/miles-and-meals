# Miles & Meals v74 — Trip Inbox

Trip Inbox accepts pasted booking text, screenshots/photos, TXT/EML and best-effort text-based PDF content. It extracts flight/hotel/train/ticket basics for review before saving.

Saved reservations can be promoted into Plan with **Add to Plan**. Scanned/image-only PDFs should be supplied as a screenshot/photo for the local OCR path.
