# V92 Living Journey Design Contract

V92 is the fixed Miles & Meals visual direction.

## Signature interaction

The four-segment Halo connects:

- **Move** — transport, flights, routes, weather and offline tickets.
- **Plan** — itinerary, tasks, free time, bookings and Companion guidance.
- **Spend** — budgets, expenses, balances, receipts and settlement.
- **People** — travelers, permissions, decisions, invitations and sync state.

The centre answers what matters now. The panel explains the relevant detail. Every state ends with a connected action.

## Fixed visual rules

- Light mode is the product identity; the previous dark Living Journey treatment is retired.
- White canvas, soft grey panels and thin borders carry operational information.
- The Halo uses `#3d82f6`, `#f5d98b`, `#edb18d` and `#9bd0a5`.
- Primary actions are black; secondary actions are white with neutral borders.
- Destination colour and imagery may add emotion but must never overpower itinerary, budget or sync information.
- Mobile uses the fixed bottom navigation; PC uses the light left rail.
- Financial and safety screens remain calm, legible and precise.
- Motion is subtle and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Functional boundary

This design layer does not change Trip permissions, expense calculations, offline queue context, financial closing, Smart Settlement, migrations or authentication.
