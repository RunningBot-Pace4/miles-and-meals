# Miles & Meals v75 — Offline 2.0

The active Trip is warmed into a device-local offline pack containing only travel essentials: trip metadata, traveler names/IDs needed for splitting, plan items and reservation essentials.

A cached offline shell can display those essentials with no network and create a Quick Expense. The expense is queued in the existing safe offline mutation queue and syncs after reconnecting. Live locations and financial balances remain server-only.
